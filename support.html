Common questions

Where is my best score kept?

Only on your phone. Deleting the game removes it. There is no account and no sign-in.

The "continue" video ad did not appear.

The ad is optional and is offered only after some crashes. It needs an internet connection. In some regions it also needs your consent to ads. If you did not agree, the game plays normally without it.

Who is the game for?

People aged 13 and over.
